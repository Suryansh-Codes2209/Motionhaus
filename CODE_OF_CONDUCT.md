# Code of Conduct

Motionhaus is a small project, and everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented) is expected to make it a place people want to come back to.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. People here have different levels of experience and different first languages.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people. Say what you think and why.
- Give and accept feedback on work in a way that helps the work.
- Credit other people's ideas and code.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour based on anyone's identity, background or beliefs.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after you have been asked to stop.
- Using the project to promote content that breaks the [Rules for using Motionhaus](RESPONSIBLE_USE.md).

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, report it privately to the maintainer through the "Report content" option on the comment or issue, or by opening a private security advisory (Security tab → "Report a vulnerability") if you cannot reach them another way. Reports are read in confidence.

## What happens

The maintainer may remove comments, code or issues that break this code, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban someone, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.
