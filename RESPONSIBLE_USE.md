# Rules for using Motionhaus

Motionhaus can find stories, write scripts, make videos and post them with nobody watching. That makes it easy to publish a lot, quickly, under your name. These rules are a condition of being part of this project, and most of them are also what YouTube, Instagram and the law already require.

## 1. Tell the truth

- Every factual claim in a video rests on a source you could show someone. Channels made from the non-launch templates enforce this (`rules.sourcesRequired`); keep it on.
- Before anything goes out, a person or the maker's own source check reads each line against its page. If the page does not say it, the line goes.
- Quotes, comments and log lines shown on screen are verbatim from a real source.
- No advice on health, money or law, and no predictions of prices, results or verdicts.

## 2. Respect other people's work

- Use only media you have the right to use. The photo search and audio library keep commercial-use licences and write the credits; publish those credits with the video.
- Do not adapt copyrighted stories, characters, games, films, manga or other creators' videos. Public-domain works, your own ideas and facts are fine.
- Do not bypass paywalls, licences or technical protections to get material.

## 3. Do not pretend to be someone else

- Do not impersonate a person, a brand or another channel, and do not use their name, logo or look to suggest they made or endorse your video.
- A channel that covers products says it is not affiliated (the launch template's credit line does this).
- Use your own channel name and artwork. The Launch Radar name and art in this repository belong to the author's channel; see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).

## 4. Be open about AI

- The voices, scripts and much of the imagery are made with AI tools. Follow each platform's disclosure rules, and turn on their AI or synthetic-media labels when the platform asks for them.
- Never make realistic footage of a real person doing or saying something they did not.

## 5. Follow the platforms' rules

- You are responsible for following the terms of YouTube, Instagram and any other platform you post to, including their rules on automated posting, spam, reused or mass-produced content, and content made for children.
- Use the official APIs with your own developer apps and your own accounts. Do not share or sell access tokens; keep them in the per-channel login files Motionhaus creates, outside the repository.
- Keep posting volume modest. One good video a day is the default for a reason.

## 6. Keep people safe

- No content that harasses, targets or exposes a private individual. Real stories from the internet are retold in your own words, with names and identifying details changed.
- No graphic violence, sexual content, hate, or dangerous instructions.
- If a channel is likely to be watched by children, mark it correctly on each platform and keep its content suitable.

## 7. Watch what runs unattended

- Start a new channel with uploads in manual mode and approve its first episodes yourself before switching to automatic posting.
- Check the notifications and the autopilot log. A channel that stops posting or posts something wrong is your responsibility to fix.
- One switch pauses everything for a channel: set `INSTAGRAM_UPLOAD_MODE=manual` in its login file.

Breaking these rules in a contribution, an issue or a discussion here can get it removed and its author blocked from the project. See also the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
