# 3d: objects in space

**Feels like:** a product launch film shot on a dark stage: every card has a visible edge, scenes sit in perspective and the camera swings slowly around them, cuts turn like the faces of a cube, a dotted floor slides toward you.
**Use for:** layered systems (a stack, an architecture with parts), 3D / spatial / game tools, renderers, anything where "how the pieces sit on each other" is the story.

**Look (automatic):** `depth` palette (near-black stage, hot orange, cool blue edge light); Sora headlines, Inter body. *Emphasis* = accent gradient. Cards are glass with an extruded edge. Every scene gets a slow orbit (`Orbit3D`). Transitions are `flip` (cube turn), zoom-punch and push. Hits are an accent flash and sparks. Captions are heavy type with an extruded edge.

## Beat sheet (30–38s: 5 beats — payoff, what it is, two "wait, really?" details, the question; see the retention rules in SKILL.md)
| # | scene | job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `hook` or `type` | the promise |
| 2 | `stack` | the system as layers, pulling apart one per beat (≤ 4, the top is the hero) |
| 3 | `spotlight` / `bento` | the thing itself |
| 4 | `stat` | traction or the speed number |
| 5 | `cta` | the question back |

## Voice
Confident, spatial words: "on top of", "underneath", "the layer that". Name each layer as it separates.

## Do / don't
- Do: `stack` layer labels ≤ 12 characters, `desc` ≤ 24; list the layers top first.
- Do: bright, high-contrast captures; the orbit dims edges.
- Don't: more than one `stack`. Don't use `tiles`, `clipping`, `log`.
- Don't: give scenes `transition: "none"`; the flip is half the style.
